Brief

Headquarters for the Luxor Order of Engineers — a contemporary civic building that draws its language from the Theban temple tradition without quoting it directly.

A massive cantilevered soffit shades the entry, its underside carrying a contemporary hieroglyphic relief. Limestone cladding catches the Upper Egyptian sun while glass insets reveal the public floors. Inside, the building organises offices, a library, and assembly spaces around a tall daylit core.

The proposal received a Recognition Award against 55+ firms competing across Egypt. I led a team of six and was responsible for 80% of the visual contribution.
