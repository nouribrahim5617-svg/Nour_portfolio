Hero render · the long porch facing the savanna

[ 01 ]

Brief

A nursery rooted in the profound ideas of remembrance, hope, and new beginnings — a building that does not just hold children, but holds the memories they will carry with them.

The design aims to create a dynamic and nurturing space that engages children through playful patterns and sensory elements, while actively planting the emotional and visual memories that will grow with them.

The architecture is meticulously crafted to leave a lasting imprint on each child, fostering a deep and joyful connection to their formative years.

[ 02 ]

Atmosphere

Patterned timber screens — drawn from local crafts — cast moving shadows across the classrooms, turning even the act of waiting into a quiet spectacle. A continuous porch holds the rooms together and provides shade from the savanna sun.
