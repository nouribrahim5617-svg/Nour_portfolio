Brief

A complete interior for a private villa in Cairo — quiet, warm, and built around the way the family actually lives.

Warm timbers, soft lime plaster walls, and carefully composed pools of light define the public rooms. Furniture is built-in where possible to keep surfaces calm and to give every wall a job to do.

[ 02 ]

Private rooms

The private wing trades the warmth of the living spaces for a calmer, more tonal palette — built-in joinery, quiet upholstery, and a single accent of colour per room.
