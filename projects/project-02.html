Brief

A competition-winning proposal for a global asylum institution — a place that gives both shelter and dignity to those seeking refuge.

The project pairs architectural strategy with a full identity system, treating the building and its visual language as a single act of welcome. Deep reds, limestone beiges and golden ochres draw on shared symbols of sanctuary; a deep blue marks moments of stillness.

The proposal was awarded 1st place at the DNSC competition.

[ 02 ]

Plans & Identity

The plan organises gathering, processing, and quiet rooms around a central light-well, so every part of the journey through the building sits under a shared sky. The accompanying brand mark — an arched aperture — appears at every scale, from a coin-sized icon to a full façade.
