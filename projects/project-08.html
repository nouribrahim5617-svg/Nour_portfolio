Brief

A community project for a small Italian town — using MVRDV's lens on density and shared programme to reframe the existing fabric.

The proposal reads the town as a sequence of courtyards, then inserts a slim public ribbon that connects church, square, and new housing. New construction is calibrated to the height and grain of the existing buildings, while the ribbon's bright interventions act as wayfinding through the town.

The project was developed in collaboration with MVRDV and Algorithm Studio during the Algorithm Rome Summer School (AANT, Italy 2024).
