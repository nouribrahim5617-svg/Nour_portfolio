Brief

A research centre that refuses to dominate its landscape — a long, low building that buries itself into the topography and reads first as ground, then as architecture.

A continuous green roof carries the building back into the campus. Glazed strips at ground level reveal labs and library; circulation is folded into a single sectional gesture so movement always pulls the eye outward to the trees.
