Aerial axonometric · the regenerated block

[ 01 ]

Brief

A re-imagining of discarded shipping containers — once symbols of disconnected trade — as the building blocks for a grounded, interdependent community.

The proposal stitches together a fragmented landscape in Al Hadath, Beirut, through architecture that prioritises restoration and social interaction. The design is less about a single aesthetic and more about using architecture as a medium for storytelling and societal change.

The core concept — Cultivating a Regenerative Community — frames the project as a seedbed: small, repeated units that, over time, grow into a living neighbourhood.

[ 02 ]

Strategy

Containers are stacked and offset to form shared roof terraces, community gardens, and shaded outdoor rooms. Reds, greens and natural timbers differentiate functions: living, planting, and gathering.
