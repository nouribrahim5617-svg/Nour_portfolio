Brief

A revival of public space across Old and New Saida — culture-led, community-centred, and built around how Saidawis actually live their evenings.

The proposal threads a series of small architectural moves through the existing fabric: a kiosk lane, a remade longitudinal section, a sequence of arched gathering spaces, and quiet repairs to the urban floor. None of the moves try to dominate — each is calibrated to fold back into Saida's rhythm.

Developed in collaboration with the Hariri Foundation / OLA.
