[ 01 ]

Brief

A comprehensive design district and art-hub in Byblos, Lebanon — addressing a varied demographic of university youth, locals, and tourists.

The programme weaves together dedicated office spaces, a state-of-the-art fabrication lab, a comprehensive material library, virtual labs, traditional libraries, and expansive collaborative spaces.

The design specifically embodies the concept of "thresholds of perception" — meticulously working with meanings and hidden contrasts so the building becomes an integrated, yet thought-provoking, element within Jbeil's historical narrative.

[ 02 ]

Atmosphere

The deep red-ochre cladding speaks to the local stone of Byblos while marking the building as a contemporary insertion. Operable timber screens filter light during the day and reveal the activity inside after dark, turning the façade itself into a record of the hub's life.

[ 03 ]

Drawings

The proposal is developed through a sequence of plans, sections and detail studies that trace the building's logic from urban gesture down to a single operable screen.
