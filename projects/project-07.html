Brief

A hospice for the terminally ill, set against the salt lakes and palm groves of Siwa Oasis — built from the materials the oasis has been using for centuries.

The proposal stitches private rooms and shaded gathering spaces around a series of natural salt pools. Adobe walls, palm-frond canopies, and a calm sequence from desert to water create a place to be still — without the institutional sterility of most end-of-life care.

The competition entry was shortlisted by Buildner in 2026 and developed during my internship at Algorithm Architects (Rome, remote).
