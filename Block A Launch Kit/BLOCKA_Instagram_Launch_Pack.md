## 1. What top architecture/archviz service pages do to get reach

**Formats that win (2026):**
1. **15–30s cinematic reels** beat stills for reach and lead conversion. Single evocative mood, not a full floor-plan tour. Seamless loops and day-to-night transitions get rewatches — rewatches per viewer are what push a reel to new audiences.
2. **Before/after transformations** (clay massing → final render, sketch → image) are the highest-converting format: they demonstrate the service in one swipe.
3. **The "lived-in look"** — steam on coffee, open books, imperfect styling. Sterile perfection is out; emotional resonance is what stops the scroll and what clients screenshot.
4. **Process/byproduct content** — ANT.archviz grew to 180k+ followers with no extra content pipeline: they posted lighting tests, material comparisons, and WIPs from paid client work. Your client projects ARE your content.
5. **List/opinion reels** ("3 things developers get wrong about renders") outperform showcase posts for follower growth; a problem-solving diagram gets shared by engineers/architects because it makes *them* look smart.
6. **Show your face** — founder-fronted posts build the trust that converts followers into clients.

**Hook mechanics:** bold claim or question in the first 1s (text overlay, not caption) · trending audio · long captions (they hold viewers while the reel loops) · strong CTA on the last slide/frame.

**Reach ≠ revenue:** viral views without a service hook bring zero clients. Every 3rd post should sell (CTA, pricing anchor, DM keyword).
