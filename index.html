[ 02 ] About
Nour Eldin Ibrahim ↪ RIBA Part 1

Architecture as a discipline that shapes human behaviour and psychology.

I'm drawn to architecture as the discipline that shapes how people feel and move — the way a threshold, a shared courtyard, or a carefully placed opening can alter a daily ritual. My work gravitates toward community-centred design: revitalising neglected public spaces, exploring the humanitarian potential of material-conscious construction, and asking who architecture is ultimately built for.

Competitions have become my primary mode of inquiry — a discipline for testing ideas beyond the studio. Across studio, internship and competition work I've collaborated with Algorithm Architects (Rome), LDRS Chehab & Partners (Beirut), Narratives Architectural Consulting (Cairo), and the Hariri Foundation.
